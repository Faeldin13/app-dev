# My Favorite Movie: *Weathering With You* (天気の子)
> *"I want you more than any blue sky."* — Hodaka Morishima

![Weathering With You Front Poster](https://images.fineartamerica.com/images/artworkimages/mediumlarge/3/weathering-with-you-hina-taki-poster-thomas-turner.jpg)

## **Overview**
**Weathering With You** is a 2019 Japanese animated romantic fantasy film written and directed by **Makoto Shinkai**. It tells the story of a runaway teenager who meets a girl with the mysterious ability to control the weather.

### Key Details
- **Director:** Makoto Shinkai  
- **Studio:** CoMix Wave Films  
- **Release Date:** July 19, 2019  
- **Language:** Japanese  
- **Runtime:** 112 minutes  
- **Genre:** Romance, Fantasy, Drama  
- **Music:** RADWIMPS

## *Plot Summary*
Hodaka Morishima escapes his rural life and arrives in Tokyo, where he meets Hina Amano, a girl who can bring sunshine with her prayers. As Tokyo faces relentless rain, the two form a bond and begin offering weather-changing services—until they discover the cost of Hina’s powers.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Characters

| Character          | Description                                                                 |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Hodaka Morishima** | A 16-year-old runaway from a remote island who arrives in Tokyo seeking freedom and purpose. He’s brave, impulsive, and deeply loyal to Hina. |
| **Hina Amano**       | A kind-hearted girl with the mysterious ability to control the weather. She becomes known as the “Sunshine Girl” and is willing to sacrifice herself to restore balance. |
| **Nagi Amano**        | Hina’s younger brother. Mature for his age, he’s charming and often gives Hodaka advice about relationships. |
| **Keisuke Suga**      | A journalist who hires Hodaka. He’s dealing with personal loss and custody issues, and serves as a mentor figure. |
| **Natsumi Suga**      | Keisuke’s niece. Energetic and supportive, she helps Hodaka navigate Tokyo and encourages his pursuit of Hina. |
| **Yasui**             | A police officer investigating Hodaka’s situation. Represents the adult world’s constraints. |
| **Takahashi**         | Another officer involved in Hodaka’s case, often seen enforcing the law during key scenes. |
